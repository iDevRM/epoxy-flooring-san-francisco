  1. A quick conversation

    We ask about the space, the floor's condition and your timeline, and answer any questions about coating systems.

  2. A free on-site visit

    We measure, look at cracks and old coatings, and run a moisture test on the slab. Most visits take 30 to 45 minutes.

    You get a clear, itemized quote and a recommended system. Nothing is booked until you approve it.
